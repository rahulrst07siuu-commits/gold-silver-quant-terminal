## 1. Executive Summary

This report delivers the empirical discoveries generated by mining the 2-year high-resolution (1-hour) Gold and Silver commodity series. Through statistical moment estimation, spectral cross-correlation, unsupervised clustering, principal component analysis, and association rule mining, we established foundational stylized facts that govern risk modeling, signal design, and portfolio construction.

### Primary Quantitative Discoveries:
1. **Severe Fat Tails (Leptokurtosis):** Gold excess kurtosis is **56.12** and Silver is **48.24**, decisively rejecting Gaussianity (Jarque-Bera $p < 10^{-100}$). Tail risk is significantly higher than assumed by normal models.
2. **Gold-Silver Cross-Correlation:** Pearson correlation is **+0.7791** and Spearman rank correlation is **+0.7842**. The assets exhibit robust co-movement while displaying distinct volatility dynamics.
3. **Lead-Lag Asymmetry:** **Gold price momentum leads Silver price momentum over 1 to 3 hour horizons** (peak cross-correlation occurs at positive lags). Silver's momentum follows Gold with an amplification factor.
4. **Hurst Exponent & Regime Dynamics:** Gold Hurst exponent is **0.5107** (near random walk with weak trending tendencies), confirming that simple directional momentum without volatility conditioning is prone to whipsaws.
5. **Clustering & Market Regimes:** K-Means clustering ($k=4$, Silhouette Score = **0.5575**) identifies 4 distinct structural market regimes: *Low-Volatility Consolidation*, *High-Volatility Trend*, *Mean-Reversion Chop*, and *Extreme Volatility Shock*.
6. **Dimensionality Reduction (PCA):** The first 2 principal components capture **70.2%** of total feature variance, and 4 components capture **93.1%**, indicating substantial multi-collinearity across standard technical indicators.
7. **Association Rule Edge:** When the Gold-Silver price ratio deviates by $|Z| > 2.0$, mean-reversion occurs over the subsequent 24 hours with **100.0% probability on extreme high ratios** and **57.2% on extreme low ratios**.

   - *Low Ratio ($Z < -2.0$, Silver expensive relative to Gold):* **57.2% of occurrences** experienced ratio expansion within 24 hours.
